About Us

Welcome to Flagship Towing!

Our mission is to deliver an affordable, friendly, and reliable on-water service while prioritizing safety, integrity, and attention to detail in all operations. The captains and staff at Flagship Towing are committed to serving our customers, bringing extensive knowledge and experience accumulated over many years on the water.

Flagship Towing specializes in assistance towing and on-water services, including jump starts, fuel delivery, removal of stuck anchors, propeller disentanglement, ungrounding's, salvage, wreck removal, vessel disposal services, storage, and impounds. Membership programs are also available for saltwater and freshwater boaters, as well as commercial businesses.

Please do not hesitate to contact our dispatch line for around-the-clock assistance. We understand that you have a choice in your on-water assistance provider, and we look forward to assisting you during your time of need.

See you on the water!
